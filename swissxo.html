GOOD TO KNOW

Your space.
Your questions.

What does self-custody mean?+

Your wallet keys are generated and held on your device. You control access to your assets and are responsible for keeping your wallet recovery information safe.

Can I add a wallet I already use?+

SwissXO supports adding existing wallets, including wallets used with Trust Wallet or MetaMask. The available assets and networks depend on the wallet and supported functionality. Ask us for a walkthrough of your use case.

Where are my conversations stored?+

Conversation history stays on your device, with no server storage of your conversations. Messages, voice and video use end-to-end encryption. Only you and the people you communicate with can read your messages.

Can I download SwissXO?+

Contact Swisstech for current access and release information. We can arrange a product walkthrough and discuss the features available to you.

Are the balances and market prices live?+

No. The screenshots illustrate the product experience. Balances are demonstration values and market prices are snapshots, not live quotes. The communication preview is an interface concept.
