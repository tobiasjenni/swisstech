A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can we start with individual modules?+

Yes. Start with the components you need and scope additional capabilities as your product develops. We’ll map dependencies and integrations around your use case.

Can the platform use our own branding?+

Yes. Web and mobile interfaces can be tailored to your identity, language and product workflows. The configuration and customisation scope is agreed before implementation.

Does Swisstech provide financial services?+

Swisstech provides the software. Banking rails, card programmes and other financial services depend on the selected providers, their approval and availability in your market.

How is SwissXO different from enterprise custody?+

SwissXO is a self-custody product with keys held on the user’s device. The platform’s crypto wallet and custody module is a separate enterprise offering with configurable custody integrations and operational controls.
