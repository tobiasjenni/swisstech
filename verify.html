A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Let’s make
it clear.

What can we screen with Verify?+

The portal brings together KYC, identity documents, proof of address, individual and business AML, crypto wallets and transactions, document forensics, and transaction monitoring.

Can we look inside the actual portal?+

Yes. Use Open portal to sign in to Verify. If your team needs access or a guided walkthrough, contact Swisstech to arrange a demo.

Can we connect Verify to our platform?+

Verify supports external references and webhook delivery tracking. Talk to us about your onboarding flow, required checks and integration scope.

Are these real customer records?+

No. The interactive previews use fictional cases and illustrative results to show the product experience. They do not display customer records or live screening results.
